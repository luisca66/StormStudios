1. WELCOME

Hi, and welcome back to Storm Studios Learning.

In this video we're going to learn how to use the sequencer, the tool you'll use to write and listen to the course exercises.

With it you can write notes and chords, listen to them, add their chord symbols and save your work.

[ON SCREEN: Harmony Course page; scroll to the bottom; «Storm Sequencer v4.0» box; click «Open v4.0»]

To open it, go to the Harmony Course page and scroll all the way down. There you'll find the Storm Sequencer box, version four. Click «Open».


2. A TOUR OF THE SCREEN

[ON SCREEN: overview; highlight each area as it is named]

Before we write anything, let's see how the screen is organized.

At the top is the transport bar. There you have the buttons to play and stop, the tempo, the cursor position, the time signature, the key and the chord symbol of the chord that's playing.

Below it are your project title and the mode. In this video we're using «Melody and chords» mode, with a single staff.

On the right are the file buttons: new project, save, open and export.

In the center is the score, where you'll write. Above it you have the note values, the mouse tools and the accidentals.

And on the right is the inspector. There you see the note you're about to write, the measure and the beat where it will go, and the editing buttons.


3. WRITING NOTES WITH THE MOUSE

[ON SCREEN: note value bar; choose «Whole»]

First I choose the note value. We have whole, half, quarter, eighth, sixteenth and thirty-second notes. I'll choose the whole note.

[ON SCREEN: «Write» tool active; click on C]

With the Write tool active, I click on the staff, on the line or space of the note I want. I'll write a C.

If the note didn't land where I wanted, I drag it up or down to the right pitch. As you move it, you hear it.

[ON SCREEN: select a note; ♯ ♭ ♮ buttons]

To add an accidental, I select the note and choose sharp, flat or natural in the accidentals bar. The Key button sets the note back to what the key signature says.

[ON SCREEN: right-click on a note]

To delete a note, I right-click it. I can also use the Eraser tool.

And if I make a mistake, no problem: Control Z undoes the last change, and Control Y redoes it.


4. WRITING CHORDS

[ON SCREEN: whole-note C written; Ctrl+click on E and on G]

Now let's write a chord. I already have a C. I hold down the Control key and click on E. The note is added to the same chord.

I do the same on G. Now we have C, E and G sounding together.

[PAUSE TO LISTEN]

[ON SCREEN: click on the E of the chord; lower it to E flat]

You can also edit a single note inside a chord. I click on the E, and only that note is selected. Now I can move it or change its accidental without touching the others.


5. LISTENING TO WHAT YOU WROTE

[ON SCREEN: Play button; then the Space key]

To listen, I press Play, or just the space bar. The same key stops it.

While it plays, a cursor moves across the score so you can follow the music.

[ON SCREEN: Tempo field, metronome, loop]

In the Tempo field you can make the music slower or faster. If you want to hear the beat, turn on the metronome. And with the loop button, the music starts over when it ends.

The start and end buttons take you to the beginning or to the last measure of the score.


6. WRITING WITH THE KEYBOARD

[ON SCREEN: «Write with keyboard» button]

If you prefer the keyboard, press «Write with keyboard». It's much faster.

The letters A through G write the notes, by their names: C, D, E, and so on.

The numbers one through six choose the note value, and the letter R writes a rest. With the Page Up and Page Down keys you change the octave.

The left and right arrows move the cursor. And if you have a note selected, the up and down arrows raise or lower it by a half step.

In the «Keyboard shortcuts» section, below the sequencer, you'll find the full list.


7. MEASURES, TIME SIGNATURE AND KEY SIGNATURE

[ON SCREEN: «Add measure» button]

When you need more room, press «Add measure». The new measure keeps the time signature and key signature of the one before it.

[ON SCREEN: «Key, meter or clef change» card in the inspector]

To change the key signature, the time signature or the clef, use the «Key, meter or clef change» card in the inspector. The change applies from the measure you choose.

[ON SCREEN: Continuous view / Page view]

You can view the score as one continuous line, or by pages, like a printed score.


8. SELECTING, COPYING AND PASTING

[ON SCREEN: Select tool; drag a rectangle over several chords]

With the Select tool, drag a rectangle over several notes to select them all.

Control C copies them, Control X cuts them, and Control V pastes them at the cursor. Control D duplicates them right after.

This is very useful in the course exercises: you write a chord or a scale once, duplicate it, and change only what you need.


9. THE PIANO ROLL

[ON SCREEN: «Piano Roll» tab; the same music appears as bars]

Besides the staff, you can see your music in the Piano Roll. Press the «Piano Roll» tab.

Here each note is a bar. Its height tells you which note it is, and its length, how long it lasts. On the left you see the name of each note, and at the top, the measures.

[ON SCREEN: click on an empty row to write a note]

To write, click on the row of the note you want, at the spot where it should start.

[ON SCREEN: drag a bar up and to the right]

To move it, drag it: up or down changes the pitch, and sideways changes when it plays.

[ON SCREEN: drag the right edge of a bar]

To change its length, grab the right edge of the bar and stretch it or shorten it.

And to delete it, just like on the staff, you right-click it.

[ON SCREEN: back to the «Staff» tab; the edited note appears in the score]

Everything you change in the Piano Roll shows up on the staff, and the other way around. They are two ways of looking at the same music.


10. CHORD SYMBOLS

[ON SCREEN: chord symbol boxes below the staff; type I, IV, V, I]

Below the staff there's a box for every beat. That's where you write the chord symbol: one, four, five, in Roman numerals, just as we do in the course.

Writing the degree of each chord helps you think about the harmony while you work, and leaves your exercise ready to review later.

The «Show harmony symbols» button shows or hides them. While the music plays, the symbol of the current chord appears in the transport bar.


11. SAVING YOUR WORK

[ON SCREEN: «Draft saved in this browser» indicator]

The sequencer automatically saves a draft in your browser. If you close the page, your work will be there when you come back.

But that draft only lives in that browser. To really keep it, press «Save JSON» and save the file on your computer.

To keep working later, press «Open project» and choose that file.

[ON SCREEN: «Export MIDI» button]

And if you want to take your music to another program, press «Export MIDI».

«New project» clears the score so you can start from scratch.


12. CLOSING

That's everything you need to get started. I recommend practicing a little: write a scale, build a few chords and listen to them.

In an upcoming video we'll see how to write for four voices: soprano, alto, tenor and bass.

See you in the next lesson.
