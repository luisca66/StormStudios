Today I have a special announcement for Storm Studios Learning: **our first app on the App Store is now available to buy**. [AP Super: Perfect Pitch](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ap-super-perfect-pitch/id6833285825) brings note recognition practice to iPhone.

## Practice you can take with you

AP Super includes 147 levels and six instrument timbres: piano, cello, French horn, choir, bassoon and guitar. Review your statistics and practice offline. It is a one-time purchase with no subscription, ads or in-app purchases.

The app supports English and Spanish and requires iOS 17 or later. Check the [official Apple listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ap-super-perfect-pitch/id6833285825) for current pricing in your region and device compatibility.

## Web training remains free

You can keep practicing with [Perfect Pitch Multi-timbral](/en/apps/oido-absoluto-multi). There is now a **Get it on the App Store** link on that page and below the web training app, so you can find the iPhone version when you want to take it with you.

This is a tool for practicing pitch recognition. Learning varies from person to person; there is no promise of developing perfect pitch within a set time.

Thank you for following the project as it grows. If you try the app and have feedback, write to me through the [contact page](/en/contact).
