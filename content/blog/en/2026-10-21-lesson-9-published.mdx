[Lesson 9](/en/harmony-course/10-lesson-9-cadences) of the [Harmony Course](/en/harmony-course) is now published in English and Spanish, with its video.

## How a phrase ends

In Lesson 6 we built four-voice chords, and in Lesson 8 we learned to connect them. Now we give them a direction: the cadence leads the phrase toward an ending, from the subdominant to the dominant and the tonic.

## What you will learn

- **The four types of cadence**: authentic (V–I), plagal (IV–I), complete (IV, II or VI – V – I) and extended (IV or II – I6/4 – V – I).
- **Inversions and the strong beat**: the last two chords are in root position, and the I6/4 of the extended cadence falls on a strong beat.
- **Half cadences**: the partial rests of the chorale, which help shape the phrase before its conclusion.

## Four cadences, four keys

The assignment is one cadence of each type, each in a different major key of your choice: 11 chords in a single file. The [Virtual Teacher](/en/virtual-teacher) recognizes the key and type of cadence and checks the chords, the inversions, the I6/4 on a strong beat, the voice leading, parallels and the leading tone.
