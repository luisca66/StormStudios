[Lesson 8](/en/harmony-course/09-lesson-8-harmonic-material-connections) of the [Harmony Course](/en/harmony-course) is now published in English and Spanish, with its video.

## Finally, connecting chords

In Lesson 6 we built four-voice chords, and in Lesson 7 we practiced how each voice moves on its own. Now we bring both together: we connect two chords. It is the last step before the first chorale.

## What you will learn

- **The harmonic material** of the major mode: which degrees we use and in which position, why six four is not used freely and why the seventh degree goes in six three.
- **The general table of connections**: which chords each one can go to, and the required continuations.
- **The connection technique**: the common tone, the six pairs of voices without parallels, and the leading tone in the soprano, which rises to the tonic.

## Eight connections, eight keys

The assignment is eight two-chord connections: I–IV, IV–V, V–I, II–V, V–VI, VI–II, I–VII6/3 and IV6/3–V, each in a different major key of your choice. The [Virtual Teacher](/en/virtual-teacher) recognizes the key of each connection and checks how the chords are built, how each voice moves, fifths and octaves, and the leading tone.
