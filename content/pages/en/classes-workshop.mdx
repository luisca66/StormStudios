## Ear training: the heart of the workshop

My **ear training course** is central to my teaching. The website's [ear training apps](/en/apps) grew out of this work: tools for practicing listening and connecting what you hear with your musical activity.

The web apps are free to use independently. If you would like to study with me, [get in touch](/en/contact) to discuss your experience and goals.

## Complete composition course

This training brings together **composition, analysis, counterpoint and orchestration**, with **audio production and music with artificial intelligence**. It is a path for developing musical ideas and working on their realization in sound.

## Film scoring

I also teach a **film scoring course**. Contact me for the syllabus, prerequisites and workshop availability.

## Instrument lessons

- Classical guitar
- Electric guitar
- Electric bass
- Drums
- Piano

## Sessions and format

Sessions last **one to three hours**. Duration, weekly frequency and format are agreed according to the course and your needs. In-person and online study are offered; contact me to confirm the format available for your chosen program.

For arrangements and availability, [contact me](/en/contact) or email [info@stormstudios.com.mx](mailto:info@stormstudios.com.mx). Tell me what you would like to study, your experience and how much time you can set aside each week.

## Resources to start studying

The website preserves and shares my educational work. You can start with the [free Harmony Course](/en/harmony-course), practice with the [apps](/en/apps) and explore the [study guides](/en/resources), whether or not you take private lessons.
