## Contact form

We request your name, email address and message to respond to your enquiry and follow up on your request. Do not include sensitive personal data or unnecessary information about others. The form does not subscribe you to a marketing list.

Resend delivers your message to the Storm Studios mailbox. Received messages remain in the mailbox to handle the conversation and follow-up. You can request deletion through the contact below, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

To prevent abuse, the server temporarily uses an identifier derived from the IP address and submission times. The contact form code does not log your message contents in application logs.

## Let me know

The email you provide through “Let me know” is used only to notify you of new apps or books. Resend delivers your request to our mailbox; we do not share your email with third parties for other purposes. Simply reply to any notification to stop receiving them.

## Progress, exercises and microphone

Completed lesson markers and various app preferences are stored in your browser. Clearing site data may remove them. The course lets you export and import completion markers; this backup does not include other apps' results.

The **Memory App** uses Firebase anonymous authentication and Firestore to store custom words associated with a user identifier. It does not require a named account or email address. Avoid including sensitive information in saved words. For a request about this data, mention the Memory App; we will help identify your record without asking for your password.

The Memory App also uses Firebase App Check with Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise to assess automated requests. Google receives technical browser and interaction data needed for that assessment. Google's [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms) apply.

Singing exercises request microphone permission. Pitch analysis takes place in the browser; these exercises do not implement uploads of voice recordings to the server. You can deny or revoke permission in your browser, although singing evaluation will not work without it.

MIDI files submitted to the **Virtual Teacher** are sent to the server for analysis using programmed musical rules. The endpoint processes files in memory and does not implement permanent MIDI storage. It logs technical evaluation information, such as the lesson, error count and score. It does not use a generative model to assess each exercise.

## Mobile apps

This section applies to Storm Studios Learning apps distributed on Google Play, such as **AP Super: Perfect Pitch**.

AP Super does not request special device permissions, does not use the microphone, does not require an account and includes no ads or third-party analytics or advertising services. Your progress and practice statistics are stored only on your device; we do not send them to any server or receive them. If you choose to export your practice history as a CSV file, you decide which app or person to share it with. Uninstalling the app deletes the data stored on the device.

Purchases and payments are handled by Google Play, which processes your payment data under its own privacy policy. Storm Studios does not receive your card details. For questions about an app, write to [info@stormstudios.com.mx](mailto:info@stormstudios.com.mx) and include the app's name.

## Website measurement

We count website visits and actions anonymously and without cookies using our own measurement in Cloudflare Workers Analytics Engine. We only record the event, public path, language, destination, referring domain and the utm_source and utm_campaign labels when present in the URL; this measurement stores no IP addresses, personal identifiers or contact form contents.

## External services and technical data

Cloudflare hosts the website and serves resources such as audio; Resend delivers messages and Firebase provides the Memory App services described above. These providers receive technical data needed to operate their services and may process it outside Mexico.

Embedded YouTube and Vimeo videos and links to external platforms are subject to those providers' policies. YouTube is embedded through its privacy-enhanced domain. Loading or playing external content may send connection data to the provider and use its storage mechanisms.

You can manage permissions and storage in browser settings. Blocking them may affect playback or saving. More information: [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/), [Resend](https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy), [Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy), [YouTube/Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Vimeo](https://vimeo.com/privacy).

## Your rights and how to exercise them

You may request access, correction, cancellation or objection to processing, withdraw consent or limit use by emailing **info@stormstudios.com.mx** with the subject **Personal data**. Include your name, a contact method, the data or service involved and your request. If identity or representation must be verified, we will indicate an appropriate method; do not send identity documents through the public form.

We will provide procedural guidance and respond within applicable legal deadlines. Withdrawal does not affect prior lawful processing and may prevent us from continuing an enquiry that requires those details.
