## From chord to motion

So far we have built isolated chords. To connect them, each voice moves like a melody. Before your first chorale, we will practice each voice on its own, because tracking everything at once is overwhelming.

In the video you will see the allowed **melodic intervals**, ascending or descending, and how they are classified, diminished intervals and successive leaps. You will also see **harmonic motion** between two voices (parallel or contrary 5ths and octaves, simultaneous leaps), which you will practice when we connect chords in the coming lessons.

## Assignment for the Virtual Teacher

Do **four exercises**, one per voice (**soprano**, **alto**, **tenor** and **bass**), and upload each one separately:

1. Choose **any major key**; it can be different in each exercise.
2. In Storm Sequencer, in SATB mode, write an **8-note melody** only on that voice's staff and leave the others empty. In **quarter notes**: two 4/4 measures.
3. The melody **begins and ends on the first degree** (the tonic).
4. Use only the **allowed intervals** and follow the rules of **melodic motion**. In this exercise **notes are not repeated**; in chord connections they will be.
5. Stay within the voice's **range**: soprano C4–G5, alto G3–D5, tenor B2–G4 and bass F2–B3.

Export the MIDI file for each voice and upload it: the Virtual Teacher recognizes the voice and the key, checks every interval, the range and the opening and closing tonic, and explains each error. You will practice harmonic motion when we connect chords.
