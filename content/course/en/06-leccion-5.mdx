## The vocabulary of harmony

Before writing in four voices we need a common vocabulary. In the video you will learn:

- The **names of the degrees**: tonic, supertonic, mediant, subdominant, dominant, submediant and leading tone (or subtonic).
- Their **functions**: tonal, modal, tendency and secondary degrees.
- What a **chord** is and how it is named by the number of stacked thirds: fifth, seventh, ninth, eleventh and thirteenth chords.
- How the **harmonic series** explains the evolution of harmony through history.
- The **tonal regions** of subdominant, tonic and dominant.
- **Tonality**, the order of accidentals and the **circle of fifths**.
