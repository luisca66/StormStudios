In this lesson we review interval theory: how intervals are named, how they are classified and how they work within the tonal system.

## Daily interval training

Intervals are one of the fundamental pillars of all musical training. Understanding their theory and names is not enough; you need to train for years both the ability to recognize them by ear and the ability to sing them accurately. Both skills are essential for developing a solid, functional musical ear.

That is why we built our [Interval Recognition](/en/apps/intervalos-reconocimiento) and [Sung Intervals](/en/apps/intervalos-cantados) apps: tools designed to accompany you every day in real ear training. Consistent daily work transforms your musical perception.
