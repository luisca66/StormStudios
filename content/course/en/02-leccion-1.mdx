## Major scales

In this lesson you learn how **major scales** are built, the foundation of all harmonic theory. We start from C major, the white keys of the piano, to discover the structure they all share:

> **whole · whole · half · whole · whole · whole · half**

Then we reproduce that structure from other notes following the **circle of fifths**: by ascending perfect fifths the sharps appear one by one, and by descending perfect fifths, the flats. You will also see why G major uses F♯ and not G♭, and how to use key signatures in the sequencer.

Before you begin, make sure you are comfortable with everything in the [Preparatory Module](/en/harmony-course): reading treble and bass clef, rhythm, intervals and using the sequencer.

## Exercise

In the sequencer, write the **15 major scales**, ascending from tonic to tonic (8 notes each):

- Ascending fifths: C · G · D · A · E · B · F♯ · C♯
- Descending fifths: F · B♭ · E♭ · A♭ · D♭ · G♭ · C♭

Export the MIDI file and upload it to the Virtual Teacher. Don't move on to Lesson 2 until your exercise is perfect.
