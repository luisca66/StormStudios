## From chord to quartet

Now we take the step toward polyphony: distributing the notes of a chord among the four voices of the **harmonic vocal quartet**: soprano, alto, tenor and bass. For now we work with isolated chords, without connecting them.

In the video you will see the **range** of each voice, the **maximum spacing** between neighboring voices, which notes can be **doubled or omitted** (the leading tone is never doubled), chord **inversions**, **melodic position** and **close or open** voicing.

## Assignment for the Virtual Teacher

In Storm Sequencer, in SATB mode, write **7 fifth chords**, one on each degree of a major scale:

1. Choose **any major key**.
2. Build all **7 degrees** (I to VII), in any order you like.
3. For each chord, freely choose its inversion, melodic position and voicing. Vary the combinations.
4. Each chord takes one 4/4 measure, with **whole notes** in all four voices.

Ranges: soprano C4–G5, alto G3–D5, tenor B2–G4 and bass F2–B3. Maximum spacing: a 12th between soprano and alto, a 10th between alto and tenor and a 15th between tenor and bass, with no voice crossings.

Export the MIDI file and upload it: the Virtual Teacher checks degrees, ranges, spacing, doublings and spelling, and describes each chord for you.
